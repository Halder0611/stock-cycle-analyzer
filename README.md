This is a cyclic stock analyzer, its pretty weird to explain what it does. well it analyzes the prices of stocks after a specific period of time 
example : AAPL, each cycle: 6 months, Total time frame: 2 years 
in this case the system will go 2 years back in time and analyses the prices every next 6 months for 2 years. (eg:increase, decrease in percentage etc) 
it was more of a personal need than a professional project.
